Everything you need to know about visiting SVV Poly Clinic and booking diagnostic tests.

Both walk-ins and advance appointments are warmly welcomed at SVV Poly Clinic. For general physician consultations and routine blood tests, you can walk in anytime between 7:30 AM and 9:30 PM. For specialized consultations (such as Ayurvedic Anorectal care or Gynecology), booking a slot online or over the phone helps guarantee minimal waiting time.
Most routine blood tests (Complete Blood Picture, Blood Sugar, Dengue/Malaria Rapid, Serum Creatinine) are processed in our in-house automated serum laboratory within 3 to 4 hours. Digital PDF reports with verifiable barcodes are automatically sent to your WhatsApp number and email. Physical printed copies can also be collected from our reception.
Yes! Our certified and vaccinated phlebotomists provide safe, hygienic doorstep blood and urine sample collections throughout Madhapur, Ayyappa Society, Arunodaya Colony, Kavuri Hills, and Hitec City. You can schedule a home visit as early as 6:30 AM via our website or WhatsApp.
